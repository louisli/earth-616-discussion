# 08 Community and open-source strategy

**Goal:** Decide what goes open, who to recruit first and how the community feeds the paid product.

## Starting points
- Open-source scope: the benchmark and arena only, or part of the engine too?
- Seed first with open-source LLM developers or prompt-engineering hobbyists?
- Return loop: a live, ongoing public leaderboard (Kaggle model), not a one-off post
- Contributed test cases and tasks, which turn contributors into evangelists
- Regular "state of prompt optimization" reports as free distribution
- Blind human voting alongside automated scores
- Flywheel: patterns the community surfaces flow into the paid definitions database
- Long-term role of the arena: community, product funnel, or both
